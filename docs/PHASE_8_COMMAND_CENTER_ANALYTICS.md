## 2. Top-Level Fleet KPIs

1. **Total Fleet:** Total registered vehicles evaluated in the database.
2. **High Risk:** Vehicles exceeding the statutory High Risk threshold ($\text{Risk} \ge 60$).
3. **Medium Risk:** Vehicles in the intermediate risk tier ($30 \le \text{Risk} < 60$).
4. **Low Risk:** Compliant, low-risk commercial fleet ($\text{Risk} < 30$).
5. **Open Cases:** Active investigation dockets (`NEW`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INVESTIGATION`).
6. **Urgent Cases:** Critical/Urgent priority investigations.
7. **Average Risk Score:** Mean population risk index ($0 - 100$).
8. **Average Compliance:** Mean statutory compliance score ($0 - 100$).

## 3. High-Risk Corridors & Toll Plazas Analytics

- **Corridor Analytics (`/api/analytics/routes`):** Groups E-Way Bills by 2-digit PIN regions to identify transit corridors with high proportions of suspicious vehicles.
- **Toll Plaza Anomaly Concentration (`/api/analytics/tolls`):** Identifies toll plazas associated with frequent speed violations and route deviations.
- **Regional Risk Ranking (`/api/analytics/regions`):** Ranks Indian States by High-Risk percentage.

---

## 4. Formal Reporting & Data Export Engine

- **Executive Intelligence Report (`GET /api/reports/executive`):** Produces executive-level summaries with KPI overviews, top risk signals, corridors, and regional rankings.
- **Vehicle Risk Dossier (`GET /api/reports/vehicle/{vehicle_number}`):** Complete vehicle evidence chain and telemetry analysis.
- **Investigation Case Dossier (`GET /api/reports/investigation/{case_id}`):** Full case history, snapshot vs live delta, evidence assessments, notes, and resolution.
- **CSV Data Exports:** Protected endpoints for exporting vehicle risk records and investigation cases.
- **Official Statutory Disclaimer:** Embedded on all reports and exports:
  > *"STATUTORY DISCLAIMER: Risk scores, ML anomaly flags, and compliance indices generated by this system are analytical signals engineered to prioritize tax audits. They do not independently establish tax evasion, fraud, or legal liability without field inspection and statutory verification."*

## 2. Key Architecture Accomplishments

1. **Phase 0 (Foundation & Baseline):** Secured SQL queries, Pydantic validation, CORS hardening, automated PyTest suite.
2. **Phase 1 (Risk Dashboard):** Multi-dimensional vehicle risk cards, compliance score, vehicle trust score, cookie-based sessions.
3. **Phase 2 (Risk Intelligence Engine):** 14-dimensional feature vector extraction, circular bearing mathematics, dynamic confidence scoring.
4. **Phase 3 (ML Anomaly Detection):** Unsupervised Isolation Forest model on multi-dimensional telemetry, 70/30 weighted Hybrid Risk engine.
5. **Phase 4 (Explainable AI & Decision Intelligence):** Factual evidence items, deduplicated risk clusters, financial exposure context, actionable audit checklist.
6. **Phase 5 (RAG + LLM Risk Copilot):** Vector store with 7 CBIC statutory chunks, cosine similarity retrieval, prompt injection defense, controlled read-only risk tools.
7. **Phase 6 (Enterprise Security & Admin Control Center):** Role-Based Access Control (`ADMIN` vs `INSPECTOR`), session revocation, last-admin protection, SHA-256 IP hashing, security headers.
8. **Phase 7 (Investigation Case Management):** Sequential case numbers (`GST-2026-NNNNNN`), immutable risk snapshot, evidence review matrix, inspector notes thread, formal resolution machine.
9. **Phase 8 (Risk Command Center & Analytics):** Top 8 KPI matrix, 30-day time series, transit corridor analytics, toll plaza telemetry, regional heatmap rankings, executive PDF/CSV exports.
10. **Phase 9 (Competition Readiness & Hardening):** Liveness & readiness health checks, Dockerization (`docker-compose.yml`, `Dockerfile.backend`, `Dockerfile.frontend`, `nginx.conf`), end-to-end integration test suite, complete documentation package.
